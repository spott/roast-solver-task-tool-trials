What this model does—and does not know

The CPU/WASM solver voxelizes an analytic shape scaled to weight, then advances temperature with lagged Choi–Okos properties and embedded-surface fluxes. Shape, oven airflow, and moisture are estimates. It does not model bones or fat separately and has not been fitted to real cooking logs.
