Model notes

Not empirically calibrated. The included fixtures are synthetic model output. This is not food-safety advice.

Implemented physics

The browser runs a Rust/WASM port of a NumPy reference: temperature-dependent lean-meat properties, 3D explicit finite-volume conduction, SDF-derived curved embedded boundaries, convection, radiation, a finite per-surface-cell moisture reservoir and crust transition, an insulated bottom pan patch, and continued ambient integration after pull.

Surface area is corrected using the SDF normal rather than counted as raw voxel faces. The energy ledger compares integrated boundary flux with body enthalpy change. The stable probe is fixed at the pull-time coldest location.

Validation

The reference suite includes a Dirichlet-box convergence mode and exact Robin sphere, slab, and cylinder series. The sphere center error is approximately 0.12% at 14 cells per radius in the tested anchor. Rust checks properties, geometry, and fixed curves against Python golden output.

Interpreting P70

The advanced value integrates 10^((Tcold−70)/7) dt. It is only an equivalent exposure for the stated reference and z-value. It is not a pass/fail result; use the correct product, organism, authority, and safety margin.

Known limits

Presets are homogeneous parametric geometry. There is no bone/fat field, WebGPU, or photo reconstruction. Oven airflow and moisture constants are engineering priors. Real calibration needs controlled probe logs.
