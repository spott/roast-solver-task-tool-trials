# Physics and numerical specification (M1–M4)

The implementation follows `PROJECT_PLAN.md`; this note records engineering choices where the plan leaves latitude.

## Units and properties

All calculations use SI units and Celsius temperature differences. Radiation converts to Kelvin. `roast_solver/properties.py` uses Choi–Okos component correlations for a 75% water / 20% protein / 4% fat / 1% ash lean-meat baseline, reciprocal density mixing, and lagged (one-step Picard) properties. This is a baseline composition model, not an empirical fit.

## Geometry and embedded boundary

Presets are a superellipsoid roast, rounded slab, teardrop ham, and a smooth union of a bird body, legs and wings minus an ellipsoidal cavity. Their common output is the SDF contract: occupancy, signed distance, outward normal, boundary area, and pan-contact mask.

Surface area is not the count of exposed voxel faces. For each sign-crossing face, projected area `h²` is assigned to the inside cell and divided by the local `|nx|+|ny|+|nz|`. This Crofton/cut-cell estimate removes the orientation bias of stair steps. The Robin interface temperature includes the SDF center-to-interface conduction resistance. Pairwise interior face powers are equal and opposite; area-weighted boundary power is the only body energy source. Therefore accumulated boundary energy and discrete sensible enthalpy change agree to floating-point precision.

The analytic sphere check uses `1 - λ cot λ = Bi`. At 32 cells across the diameter, its center ratio error at `Bi=0.5, Fo=0.2` is below 0.1% and area error about 1%; the permanent acceptance target is below 1% center error at production resolution. An independently discretized infinite-slab geometry is checked against `λ tan λ = Bi`: error decreases by more than 5× from 8 to 24 cells through thickness, is below 0.2% at production resolution, and remains below 0.5% for a high-transfer `Bi=20` water-bath anchor. This high-Bi check serves the same interior-model isolation role as published sous-vide tables without presenting a synthetic model run as measured validation.

## Boundary stages

Convection is 10 W/m²K still or 20 W/m²K fan by default. Radiation uses the exact-factor lagged linear coefficient. Evaporation computes a Lewis-analogy mass-transfer potential, capped by heat available above a configurable wet-bulb plateau. Every surface cell owns a moisture reservoir (synthetic baseline 0.30 kg/m²). Depletion switches that cell to a 6% dry-crust rate. Covered mode multiplies evaporation by 0.02. The lowest downward-facing SDF patch is insulated as pan contact.

At pull, the solver records the coldest cell as a stable probe, swaps to room convection/radiation, optionally applies low-emissivity/reduced-convection foil properties, and continues through rest. Carryover is the stable probe's peak minus pull temperature. Pasteurization is the displayed equivalent time integral `∫10^((T-70°C)/10°C)dt`; it is a model output, **not food-safety advice**.

## Calibration status

`fixtures/synthetic_roast_v1.json` is intentionally synthetic. It is generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_fixture.py` from this model to detect regressions and demonstrate the future fixture shape. It contains no probe measurements and provides no empirical calibration or accuracy claim. Real logs would be required to fit convection, emissivity and reservoir values by preset.
